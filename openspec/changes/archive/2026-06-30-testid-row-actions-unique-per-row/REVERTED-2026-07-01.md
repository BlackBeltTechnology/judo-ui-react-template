# PARTIALLY REVERTED 2026-07-01

This change was archived on 2026-06-30 and then partially reverted on
2026-07-01 (commit `5dea8679`, on branch
`feature/JNG-6391_Test_Data-TestId`).

## What is KEPT (F11 — row-unique per-row testids)

The row-unique `data-testid` composition on per-row action buttons
remains in the shipped code exactly as archived here:

- The `buildRowTestId(actionId, rowIdentifier)` helper in
  `components/table/table-row-actions.tsx.hbs`.
- Row-unique `data-testid` composed at render time on split-action
  `<Button>`, `DropdownButton` triggers, and dropdown menu items via
  `buildRowTestId(a.id, params.row.__identifier)`.
- The `testId?: string` prop on `DropdownButton` (with fallback
  `testId ?? id`), which callers use to pass the composed per-row
  id through to the trigger button and each menu item.

The `table-row-actions-testids` capability under `openspec/specs/`
therefore remains in place and continues to describe this behavior.

## What is REVERTED (F10-widening — selector-mount testid strip)

The "F10-widening" half of the archived change stripped `data-testid`
emission on baseline checkbox, toolbar buttons, per-row split action
buttons, and DropdownButton triggers / menu items when the table was
mounted inside an AddSelector / SetSelector dialog overlay. On
review this was rejected in favor of an eventual per-mount test-id
prefix (extending the F11 row-id composition pattern to portal
mounts) rather than DOM-level suppression.

The revert commit removed:

- The `isSelectorMount?: boolean` parameter on
  `ColumnActionsProvider` (in `utilities/interfaces.ts.hbs`) and on
  `columnsActionCalculator` (in
  `components/table/table-row-actions.tsx.hbs`); the calculator now
  unconditionally emits `buildRowTestId(...)` at every call site.
- The `suppressTestId?: boolean` prop on `DropdownButton` and its
  `DropdownMenuItem` interface, plus the derived `triggerTestId`
  ternary; the trigger and every menu item now always emit
  `testId ?? id`.
- The `testIdPrefix === undefined/null` early-return in
  `CustomCheckbox` (in `components/table/SelectCheckbox.tsx.hbs`);
  the wrapper now always emits a `data-testid` attribute.
- The `containerIsSelector ? undefined : uniqueId` and
  `isSelectorTable ? undefined : uniqueId` ternaries on
  `baseCheckbox.testIdPrefix` in `LazyTable.tsx.hbs` and
  `EagerTable.tsx.hbs`.
- The `containerIsSelector ? undefined : toolBarAction.id` and
  `isSelectorTable ? undefined : toolBarAction.id` ternaries on the
  toolbar `<Button data-testid=...>` in both tables.

The `table-toolbar-rendering` capability under `openspec/specs/`
(created by the archival step of this change) was deleted in the
same revert commit, since its broadened requirement enumerated
these five strip sites.

The `table-row-actions-testids` spec was edited to remove the
paragraph that referenced `table-toolbar-rendering`'s precedence
rule ("when `isSelectorMount === true`, no `data-testid` is emitted
regardless of row identifier"). The three scenarios covering F11
behavior are unchanged.

Snapshots for the 2 affected files (in ActionGroupTestPro) were
regenerated via `-DforceSnapshotOverwrite=true` and the full itest
suite built green.
